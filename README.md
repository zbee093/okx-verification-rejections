# OKX verification: how to complete identity checks, avoid common rejections, and understand regional limits

Searching for “OKX verification” can mean two different things: verifying the email or phone number used to create an account, or confirming your identity so you can use account services. This guide focuses on identity verification, often called KYC. The steps and supported documents depend on where you live, so check that OKX serves your location before starting. In the United States, for example, services vary by state, and OKX says U.S. residents must use OKX US rather than services offered by its non-U.S. entities.

## What OKX verification involves

Identity verification asks you to provide personal details and, where required, identity documents, a selfie or facial check, and proof of address. The information may include your country of residence, nationality, full legal name, date of birth, ID number, document type, and residential address. The exact steps and accepted documents depend on the region selected in your account.

That regional detail matters. Citizenship alone does not determine eligibility: OKX says your country of residence and the local entity operating there are key factors. Even where registration is available, some products or features may be restricted.

Identity verification is also separate from the email and phone checks used during account registration. Those confirm control of your contact details; they don’t replace the identity check.

## Which documents should you prepare?

OKX says it accepts valid government-issued IDs from eligible countries, with accepted types depending on regional requirements. Its examples include passports and driving licences, but the available choices in your verification flow are the ones that matter for your location. Don’t assume that a document accepted in one country will be accepted in another.

Before uploading, check that the document:

- Is valid and not expired.
- Shows the requested information clearly, including your name and date of birth.
- Is photographed sharply, without glare or blur.
- Has all required edges or corners visible.
- Is an original document image, not a screenshot.

If proof of address is requested, use a document showing your current residential address and follow the date requirements displayed for your region. OKX’s general guidance says documents must be issued within the last six months for EEA users and within three months for other regions. The verification screen may provide additional local requirements.

For a selfie, keep your face fully inside the frame and unobstructed. Hats, masks, glasses, poor lighting, or flash glare can make the image harder to match. The goal is a clear, current image that follows the instructions on screen, not a carefully staged passport portrait.

## How long does OKX verification take?

OKX says document processing can take about 30 seconds after upload. If the application needs additional review, it may take up to 24 hours. These are estimates, not guaranteed completion times; OKX notes that demand can extend waits. It says it will send confirmation by SMS or email and recommends contacting support if there’s no update after 24 hours.

Keep an eye on the verification status in your account and check the contact details attached to it. Re-submitting the same application repeatedly is unlikely to help if the issue is an unsupported document or mismatched personal details.

## Why can OKX verification fail?

A rejection or stalled application doesn’t always mean there is one single problem. OKX lists several possible causes, including duplicate identity verification, an unsupported or expired ID, unreadable photos, mismatched profile details, and problems with facial recognition or camera access. It also identifies unsupported countries or regions and not following the on-screen process as possible issues.

Work through the likely causes methodically:

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Document photo rejected | Retake it in good light; keep the full document in frame and the text sharp. |
| Details don’t match | Compare your account’s name, birth date, and other details with the document. Correct errors through the account’s supported process. |
| ID already used | Check whether you already have another OKX account. OKX flags duplicate identity verification as a possible cause. |
| Face check fails | Use a clear, well-lit image, keep your face unobstructed, and follow the framing guide. |
| Camera does not open | Check the app’s camera permission in your device settings. |
| Document type unavailable | Confirm your selected country or region and choose only from the document types the verification flow offers. |
| Upload stalls or buttons do not respond | Check your connection, update the app, and retry. OKX also recommends reinstalling the app if issues persist. |

These steps can help with common technical or image-quality problems, but they can’t override eligibility rules or guarantee approval. If the account gives a specific reason, use that message to decide what to fix. For account-specific cases, contact OKX through its support channel rather than sending identity documents to someone claiming to help through social media.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I verify OKX without an identity document?

The process may vary by country, but OKX’s individual verification instructions require identity information and describe valid government-issued IDs for eligible regions. Use the options shown in your account; don’t substitute unofficial methods or someone else’s documents.

### Can I use a passport instead of a driving licence?

Possibly. OKX lists passports and driving licences as examples of government-issued IDs, but accepted types are determined by your region. Choose a document type that appears in your verification flow.

### What if I live in the U.S.?

Check that OKX serves your state and use the U.S. service and terms that apply to your residence. OKX distinguishes OKX US from its non-U.S. entities and says U.S. users are prohibited from accessing foreign products and services.

### My application has been pending for more than a day. What should I do?

Check your account status and email or SMS notifications. OKX says additional review can take up to 24 hours and advises contacting its Support Center or chatbot if you have not received an update after that period.

### Can I submit another person’s ID?

No. The verification should use the account holder’s own accurate identity information. A mismatch between the account and submitted documents can lead to rejection; OKX also identifies duplicate identity verification as a possible issue.

### Does verification guarantee access to all OKX products?

No. Availability depends on where you reside, applicable restrictions, and the specific product. A verified account can still have regional limits.

## The practical takeaway

For a smooth OKX verification attempt, confirm regional availability first, use the identity document offered for your residence, enter details exactly as they appear on it, and upload clear original images.
